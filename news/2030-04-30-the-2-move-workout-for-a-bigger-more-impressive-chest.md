# The 2-Move Workout For a Bigger, More Impressive Chest

If you want to start filling out your shirts more, you need to make your upper pecs pop. This two-move routine is designed to do just that.

The first move is a single-joint, isolation exercise that zeroes in on your upper pecs, pre-fatiguing them.

[Bigger More Impressive Chest](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/)

[Workout For Bigger More](https://bioresearch.wiki/ba7mps55dk/)

Then you immediately move into the second exercise: a multi-joint, compound move that enlists the help of your triceps to allow your already burning pecs to stay in the muscle-building fight longer than they normally could.

You’ll really “feel” your upper pecs workout throughout this superset as a result. That enhanced mind-muscle connection—combined with your increased time under tension—will help you achieve serious gains.
