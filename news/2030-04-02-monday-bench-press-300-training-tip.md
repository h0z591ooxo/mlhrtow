# Monday Bench Press & 300 Training Tip

A lot of people want to know how often they should do the 300 workout, or how to train for it.

[Monday Bench Press Training](https://therapy.pepgen.cc/tag/naturium-natural-peptide/)

Well, as the creator of the workout says in the Men's Health article, the actors didn't do this workout every day. It was just a "rite of passage" done once at the end of the training period. So follow his rules, and if you want to do it, just try it once. Maybe you can do it every couple of months, but don't train like that every day.

[Monday Bench Press Training](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/)

If you want to practice for it, before you do it full-on, you might want to add one of the events to the end of your regular workout, like I have today, by doing 50 deadlifts with 135 pounds on the bar. Last week I did one set of 35, and then 1 set of 15. This week, I upped the ante a little.
