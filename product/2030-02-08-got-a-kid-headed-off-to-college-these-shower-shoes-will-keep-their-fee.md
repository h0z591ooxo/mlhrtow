Got a Kid Headed Off to College? These Shower Shoes Will Keep Their Feet Healthy

So, you’re off to college and making your list of what to bring to your new dorm. Microwave? Check. Mini fridge? Check. Mattress topper? Definite check. Shower shoes? Yeah, you don’t want to forget those. We promise.

Also commonly called shower slippers or shower slides, shower shoes are an important part of foot hygiene, whether you’re off to college or the gym.

“[They’re] very beneficial, especially for people who share bathing spaces like in pool locker rooms, gyms, or in dorms,” says Samantha Landau, DPM, MPH, assistant professor of orthopedics and pediatrics at Touro New York College of Podiatric Medicine in New York City. “This is all to prevent someone from being barefoot in a communal space that is prone to the transmission of many different types of infections.”

What to Look for When Buying Shower Shoes

Shower shoes come in all shapes and sizes, and they all have different features that make some more appealing than others.

Though you can likely pick from a variety of flip-flops or slides for a shower slipper, here are a few key factors to take into consideration:

Do the shoes fit well? Shoes that are too big or too small are not only uncomfortable but may also make it harder to keep your balance on a wet surface. It’s important that your shoes fit correctly and are not slippery, as these factors can increase the risk of falling, especially in a wet shower.

What type of material are the shoes made of? “Shower shoes that contain materials such as EVA, rubber, or waterproof plastic are able to dry quickly [and] resist moisture absorption,” says Lazar.

Can you easily clean and disinfect the shower slipper? Some shower shoes are washing machine-safe and can be left to air-dry. Others can be easily cleaned with soap and water, and then disinfected with wipes or a bleach-based solution. Carefully read the instructions for keeping your specific shoes clean and disinfected.

What features does the shoe have to help keep me from falling? “Look for non-slip soles with a good grip,” says Dr. Kormeili. It’s important that your shoes can keep you stable on wet surfaces. Be sure to determine whether the shoe provides enough traction and stability for you.

Are There Drawbacks to Wearing Shower Shoes?

If you don’t care for your shower shoes properly, there are a few ways it could be detrimental to your foot hygiene:

Shoes that aren’t cleaned can harbor bacteria and germs.

You can slip and fall in shoes that don’t fit well or don’t have enough traction for your needs.

You may encounter problems if the shoes are not made from a quality material. Shower shoes “can wear out quickly if not made with substantial materials, [or they] can trap moisture and not dry quickly if not made with quality materials,” notes Lazar.

FAQ

You’ll know it’s time to replace your shower shoes if they start to peel or break, you notice an odor, or it takes too long for them to dry.

Also, if you find that you’re slipping or having trouble maintaining your balance when wearing them, consider getting another pair of shower shoes.

“Shower shoes are great essentials, but when used beyond their life span or not cleaned properly, they can come with some problems, such as buildup of odor or bacteria, slipping due to loss of foot grip, and sometimes even as bad as foot infections,” Lazar concludes.
