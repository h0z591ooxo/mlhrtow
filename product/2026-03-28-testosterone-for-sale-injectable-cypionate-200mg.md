What is Testosterone Prescribed For?

Testosterone is prescribed for hypogonadism, normal male development, and low testosterone levels. Hypogonadism is when your body doesn’t produce enough testosterone.

How Does Testosterone Replacement Therapy Work?

TRT supplements the body’s natural testosterone levels when they are insufficient due to conditions such as hypogonadism (low testosterone production).
