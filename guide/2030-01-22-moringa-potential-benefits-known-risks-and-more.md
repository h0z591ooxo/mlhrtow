The Takeaway

Moringa leaf is generally considered safe to incorporate into your diet. While studies suggest that it may support your immune system, reduce inflammation, improve blood sugar, and support weight loss, more research is needed in order to have conclusive evidence of these purported health benefits.

While fresh moringa leaves are eaten in some countries, powdered or dried moringa leaves are the most common forms of the supplement in the U.S., and can be used in smoothies or tea.

Talk to your healthcare team before using moringa, especially if you’re pregnant, breastfeeding, or taking a medication that may interact with moringa.
