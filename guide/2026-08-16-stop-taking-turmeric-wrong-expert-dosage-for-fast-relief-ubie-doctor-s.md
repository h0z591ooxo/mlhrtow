Science-Backed Dosage Guidelines

Clinical trials vary in dose, formulation, and length of use. Here's what research suggests for turmeric for inflammation dosage:

1. Standardized Curcumin Extracts

Most studies use higher concentrations of curcumin rather than plain turmeric powder:

500 mg to 1,000 mg of curcumin per day, divided into two doses.

Typical trial duration: 4 to 12 weeks to notice meaningful improvements.

Some studies extend up to 6 months for chronic conditions like osteoarthritis.

Whole turmeric root powder contains about 3% curcuminoids. To reach therapeutic curcumin levels:

1,200 mg to 3,000 mg of turmeric powder daily, split into two or three doses.
