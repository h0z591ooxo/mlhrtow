Working with Your Doctor: Why Medical Guidance Matters

Self-experimenting with supplements can be risky. A doctor can help you:

Assess medical history (liver, kidney, heart function).

Order baseline labs (NAD+ metabolites, liver enzymes, complete blood count).

Monitor response and adjust dosing.

Spot potential interactions (with medications like niacin, statins, or anticoagulants).

Optimizing Dosing and Timing

There's no one-size-fits-all dose. Work with your doctor to find the sweet spot:

Oral NR/NMN:• Common range: 250–500 mg once or twice daily• Timing: Morning or pre-workout to support energy

IV NAD+:• Typical protocols: 250–500 mg per session, 1–2 times per week• Follow-up labs: Check NAD+ metabolite levels after initial sessions

Sublingual:• 100–200 mg lozenge, 1–2 times daily• Use before training or in the afternoon slump

Tips for maximizing benefits:

Take with a small meal or healthy fat to improve absorption.

Stay hydrated—NAD+ turnover can increase water needs.

Balance with sleep, nutrition, and periodized training.

Supporting Lifestyle Practices

NAD+ is most effective when paired with healthy habits:

Nutrition• High-quality protein for muscle repair• Antioxidant-rich fruits and vegetables (berries, leafy greens)• Healthy fats (olive oil, nuts)

Sleep• Aim for 7–9 hours nightly• Maintain consistent sleep-wake times

Training Periodization• Alternate hard, moderate, and easy sessions• Include active recovery (yoga, light cycling)

Stress Management• Mindfulness, deep breathing, meditation• Balance work, life, and training demands

Monitoring Progress and Safety

Keep a close eye on how you feel and what your labs show:

Performance metrics: Track PRs, perceived exertion, recovery times.

Subjective measures: Energy levels, sleep quality, mood.

Lab tests: • NAD+ metabolites (NAD+, NADH, NMN) • Liver and kidney function panels • Inflammatory markers (CRP, creatine kinase)

Report any unusual symptoms—headache, flushing, digestive upset—to your doctor promptly. Regular check-ins ensure you're on the right track.

Potential Side Effects and Contraindications

Most people tolerate NAD+ boosters well, but be aware of:

Mild flushing or skin warmth (especially with high niacin doses)

Headache or mild nausea

Sleep disturbances if taken late in the day

Serious reactions are rare but can include rapid heart rate or allergic response. Stop supplementation and seek immediate medical attention if you experience:

Chest pain or tightness

Severe dizziness or fainting

Signs of anaphylaxis (rash, swelling, difficulty breathing)

Racing/PR Days• Take a sublingual dose 30–60 minutes pre-event• Hydrate well and follow your usual warm-up routine

Post-event, resume your recovery protocol: active cooldown, protein intake, and rest.
