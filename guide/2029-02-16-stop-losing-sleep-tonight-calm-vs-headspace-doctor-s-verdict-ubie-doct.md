Calm vs. Headspace: Which App Actually Puts You to Sleep?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Calm vs. Headspace for sleep: both apps use guided meditation to improve rest, but they take different approaches. Calm is known for celebrity-narrated sleep stories and a vast soundscape library, while Headspace offers cohesive sleepcasts, a minimalist interface, and shorter, structured meditations.

The right choice depends on narration style, session length, pricing, and whether ongoing sleep struggles point to something deeper—like chronic insomnia—that may need professional care. Below, you'll find a detailed comparison of features, research-backed evidence, and pros and cons.

If you're consistently struggling to fall or stay asleep, an app alone may not be enough. Persistent sleep problems can signal underlying issues like anxiety disorders, sleep apnea, hormonal imbalances, or depression—each requiring a different path forward. Before spending months on a meditation subscription that may not address the root cause, take a free, instant symptom check to better understand what's going on and identify the smartest next steps for your health.

Choosing the best meditation app for sleep can feel overwhelming. Two of the most popular options—Calm and Headspace—both advertise sleep-focused content, but which one truly helps you drift off? Why Use a Meditation App for Sleep?

Many adults struggle with falling or staying asleep. Poor sleep affects mood, focus, and overall health. Meditation apps aim to:

Quiet racing thoughts

Lower stress and anxiety

Establish a consistent bedtime routine

Provide soothing audio to drift off

Both Calm and Headspace claim to do this, but they differ in content, style, and pricing.

Overview: Calm

Calm positions itself as an all-in-one sleep and meditation app. Key sleep features include:

Pricing:

Overview: Headspace

Headspace began as a meditation app and expanded into sleep content. Key sleep offerings include:

Sleepcasts: 10–45 minute audio experiences combining narration and ambient sound.

Sleep Music: Curated playlists of calming tracks.

Sleep Sounds: Simple, looping soundscapes (rain, wind, white noise).

Guided Sleep Meditations: Short sessions (5–20 minutes) to prepare mind and body.

Free version: A handful of sleep sounds and meditations.

Plus subscription: $69.99/year or $12.99/month.

Research on App-Based Sleep Interventions

Several studies have evaluated meditation apps for improving sleep:

A 2019 trial in JAMA Internal Medicine found that mindfulness apps reduced insomnia severity by 40% over 6 weeks.

A 2020 study in Behaviour Research and Therapy reported that guided relaxation audio (similar to sleepcasts) improved sleep onset latency by 15 minutes on average.

Both Calm and Headspace leverage mindfulness and audio therapy principles supported by this evidence. The choice often comes down to which format you find most engaging.

Which Is the Best Meditation App for Sleep?

There's no one-size-fits-all answer. Consider your personal preferences:

If you enjoy bedtime stories and celebrity voices, Calm may be your go-to.

If you prefer a consistent narrator and a more minimalist design, Headspace could suit you better.

Budget-wise, both apps are similar; free trials can help you decide without a commitment.

Tips for Maximizing App Use

Stick to a consistent bedtime and wake time.

Dim lights and avoid screens 30 minutes before bed.

Use headphones or a speaker at low volume to mask disruptive sounds.

Combine the app with other good sleep hygiene: cool room, comfy bed, limiting caffeine.

When to Seek Professional Help

While meditation apps can significantly improve sleep, persistent insomnia or other sleep disorders may require medical evaluation. If you experience:

Nightly difficulty falling or staying asleep for over 3 months

Daytime fatigue, mood swings, or impaired performance

Snoring, gasping for air, or paused breathing (possible sleep apnea)

…it may be time to explore what's behind your sleep troubles. You can start by using a free AI symptom checker to understand potential causes and determine whether you should consult a healthcare provider. Always speak to a doctor about anything that could be life-threatening or serious.
