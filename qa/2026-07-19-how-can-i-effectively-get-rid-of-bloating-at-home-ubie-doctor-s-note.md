Bloating can be uncomfortable, but there are ways to manage it at home. One effective approach is to change your diet. Foods high in FODMAPs, which are certain types of carbohydrates, can lead to bloating. These include foods like beans, onions, and wheat products. By reducing these foods, you might see an improvement.

Additionally, some people find relief by following a gluten-free diet. This can be especially helpful if you are sensitive to gluten. Remember, everyone is different, so it might take some time to find what works best for you. Make sure to drink plenty of water and eat slowly to help your digestion. If bloating continues, it might be helpful to talk to a healthcare professional.
