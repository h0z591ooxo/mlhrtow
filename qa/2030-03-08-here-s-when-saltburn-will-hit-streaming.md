Set against the backdrop of a labyrinthine country house in the summer of 2007, it stars Barry Keoghan as Oliver, a lonely young man who becomes increasingly obsessed with his charismatic friend Felix (Jacob Elordi) and his aristocratic family's decadent lifestyle.

There's not a lot more that can be said about the plot of Saltburn without getting into spoiler territory, but its lurid visuals and dark sense of humor are charming critics, as are Keoghan and Elordi's central performances—which are supported by stellar, scene-stealing work from Rosamund Pike, Carey Mulligan, Richard E.
