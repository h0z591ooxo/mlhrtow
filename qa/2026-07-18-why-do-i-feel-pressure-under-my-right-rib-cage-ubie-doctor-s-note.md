There are several reasons why you could feel pressure under your right rib cage. One common cause is related to muscular causes. When muscles around the rib cage are strained or stretched, they might produce a feeling of pressure. Another possibility is indigestion or acid reflux, where stomach acid moves up into the chest and can cause a sensation of pressure or discomfort. Additionally, your liver and gallbladder are located under the right rib cage. Issues like gallstones can cause pain in this area. If the pressure continues, worsens, or is accompanied by other symptoms like fever or changes in stool, it’s a good idea to talk with a healthcare provider.
