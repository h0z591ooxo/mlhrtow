People with diabetes are significantly more likely to develop stiffness, inflammation, and restricted range of motion in the shoulder joint.

Explanation

If you find it hard to lift your arm away from your body, it might be due to a few reasons. One possible cause is related to diabetes. Diabetes can make someone more likely to develop a condition called 'frozen shoulder,' where the shoulder becomes stiff and painful, limiting movement.

Another reason could be problems with the muscles that help lift your arm. These muscles, called arm abductor muscles, are important for moving your arm up and out. If these muscles are weak or injured, it might be hard to lift your arm away from your body.

While these are some possible causes, it's important to talk to a healthcare professional to understand what might be happening in your specific case.
