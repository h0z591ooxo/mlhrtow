Tonsillitis is often caused by contagious viruses and bacteria—such as the Epstein-Barr virus (EBV), streptococcus, and common cold viruses—that are transmitted through saliva and respiratory droplets. Activities that involve exchanging saliva, including kissing, sharing drinks, using the same utensils, or sharing toothbrushes, can easily pass these germs from person to person.
