Explanation

Eating certain foods, especially dairy products, may be linked to acne. Some research suggests that dairy, like milk and cheese, might make acne worse. This is because dairy can affect hormones in the body that may lead to more acne.

Key Points:

Dairy Products: Studies have shown that people who drink more milk or eat more cheese might have more acne. The exact reason isn't fully known, but it could be related to hormones found in milk.
