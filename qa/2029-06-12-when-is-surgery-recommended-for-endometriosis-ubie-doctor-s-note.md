Surgery for endometriosis is usually considered when other treatments, like medications, aren’t working well enough.

When is Surgery Recommended?

Severe Pain: If the pain from endometriosis is very severe and doesn't get better with medicine, surgery may be needed.

Fertility Problems: If a woman is having trouble getting pregnant because of endometriosis, surgery may help.

[for endometriosis Ubie Doctor's](https://lifegoesom.com/htifrc/)

Types of Surgery

Laparoscopic Surgery: This is a common type of surgery for endometriosis where doctors make small cuts and use a camera to see inside the pelvis and remove endometriosis tissue.

Benefits

Surgery can help improve pain and improve the chances of getting pregnant for some women.

It's important to talk to a doctor to understand if surgery is the right choice. They will consider how bad the symptoms are and what other treatments have been tried. Surgery is one option, especially when other treatments do not help enough.
