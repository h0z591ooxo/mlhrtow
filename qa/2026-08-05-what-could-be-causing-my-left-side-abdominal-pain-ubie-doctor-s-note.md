Explanation

Left side abdominal pain can happen for many reasons. It might be due to problems with your digestive system, like constipation or an upset stomach. Sometimes, it could be because of issues with organs like the spleen or kidneys.

Possible Causes:

Digestive Issues: This includes constipation, gas, or inflammation in the intestines.

Kidney Problems: Pain might come from kidney stones or infections.

Spleen Issues: Sometimes, the spleen can become enlarged or injured.

What to Do:

See a Doctor: It's important to visit a healthcare provider to find out what's causing the pain.

Tests: The doctor might do tests like an ultrasound to look inside your belly.
