Does Insurance Cover IV Therapy?

Insurance providers generally cover IV therapy when it is deemed medically necessary. Such treatments are often prescribed for serious health conditions and administered under the supervision of healthcare professionals.

Insurance providers are less likely to cover IV therapy when it is used for non-essential, elective, or wellness purposes.

Understanding the distinction between medically necessary and elective IV therapies is crucial for navigating insurance coverage. Always consult with your healthcare provider and insurance company to determine the coverage specifics for your situation.

IV therapy can be covered by an insurance policy when it is medically necessary. Examples of when IV therapy is medically necessary includes these situations:

Dehydration and Fluid Replacement

IV fluids are commonly used to treat moderate to severe dehydration resulting from illness, heat exhaustion, or surgery.[1] This form of rehydration is a standard medical procedure and is typically covered by insurance plans.

Intravenous Immunoglobulin (IVIG) Therapy

IVIG therapy involves administering antibodies to patients with weakened immune systems or autoimmune disorders[2]. While the treatment can be costly, many insurance plans cover it, especially when prescribed for conditions like primary immunodeficiency diseases (PIDD).
