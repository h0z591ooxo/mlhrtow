# Throat Swelling Worse at Night? Warning Signs & Urgent Causes | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

What could cause throat swelling that worsens at night?

Internal Medicine

Throat swelling that worsens at night is often linked to conditions such as snoring-induced angioedema, allergic reactions, acid reflux (GERD), postnasal drip, or sleep apnea. Lying flat can increase pressure and inflammation in the throat, making symptoms more noticeable overnight.

Explanation

Throat swelling that gets worse at night can be caused by several conditions. One possible cause is snoring-induced vibratory angioedema, where the vibrations from snoring lead to swelling in the throat area. This happens because the tissues in the throat vibrate during snoring, causing them to become swollen over time.

Another potential cause is angioedema, which is a sudden swelling under the skin. It can occur due to an allergic reaction, certain medications, or even without a known cause. Angioedema can be serious and may require immediate medical attention, especially if it affects breathing.

Additionally, conditions like Ludwig's angina can cause throat swelling. Ludwig's angina is a serious infection that affects the floor of the mouth and can lead to swelling that might worsen at night due to lying down, which can increase pressure in the area.

If you or someone you know is experiencing throat swelling, especially if it worsens at night, check your symptoms now to help identify potential causes and determine whether immediate medical attention is needed.
