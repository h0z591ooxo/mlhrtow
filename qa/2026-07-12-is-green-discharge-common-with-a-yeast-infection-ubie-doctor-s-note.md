[common with yeast infection](https://healingkinetics.bbsxyxy.com/)

A yeast infection usually causes a thick white discharge that might look like cottage cheese, and it often comes with itching or irritation. Green discharge is not something you normally see with yeast infections. When someone has green discharge, it might be a sign of another condition or infection, such as certain sexually transmitted infections or bacterial infections. If you notice green discharge or any change in your usual discharge pattern, it is important to talk with your healthcare provider.
