In early pregnancy, many women experience mild abdominal aches or cramps. These symptoms occur because your body undergoes significant changes while supporting new life. The hormones of pregnancy cause muscle relaxation and digestive changes, which can lead to stomach discomfort, nausea, and acid reflux. Mild cramping is normal as your uterus grows and expands. However, contact your doctor if you experience severe stomach pain, prolonged discomfort, or additional symptoms like heavy bleeding, fever, severe nausea, or vomiting as these symptoms could indicate a serious condition or early pregnancy complication. Monitor your symptoms closely and inform your care provider about any concerning changes. During pregnancy, it's important to discuss with your healthcare provider what's normal for your situation—this ensures you receive appropriate care and guidance.
