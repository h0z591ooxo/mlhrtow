How to Identify if Shilajit Is Triggering Your Immune System

Shilajit is a natural resin used for centuries in traditional medicine. It's praised for its potential to boost energy, support cognitive function, and promote general wellness. However, because Shilajit can modulate the immune system, some people—especially those with autoimmune conditions—may experience unexpected reactions or flares. This guide will help you spot signs that Shilajit is stimulating your immune system, monitor your response safely, and make informed decisions.

Understanding Shilajit's Impact on Immunity

Shilajit contains fulvic acid, minerals, and bioactive compounds believed to support cellular health.

Research suggests these compounds can enhance immune activity by increasing antioxidant defenses and regulating inflammatory pathways.

For most people, gentle immune stimulation is beneficial. But if you have an autoimmune condition (e.g., rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, Hashimoto's thyroiditis), heightened immune activity may worsen symptoms or trigger flares.

Key term: Shilajit and autoimmune flares—people with autoimmune disorders should pay close attention to any changes in symptoms after starting Shilajit.

When to Seek Professional Help

Most mild immune stimulations subside within a few days. However, contact your doctor if you experience:

High fever (above 101°F/38.3°C)

Severe joint or muscle pain that limits function

Difficulty breathing or chest pain

Worsening of known autoimmune markers (e.g., thyroid swelling, skin ulcerations)

Neurological changes (seizures, significant numbness)
