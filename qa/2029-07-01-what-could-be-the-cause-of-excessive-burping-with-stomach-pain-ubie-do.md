Explanation

Excessive burping, also known as belching, along with stomach pain can be uncomfortable. One common cause is swallowing too much air, known as aerophagia. This can happen when eating or drinking too quickly, chewing gum, or smoking. Another possible cause is gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), where stomach acid flows back into the food pipe, causing pain and burping as seen in Reference 2.
