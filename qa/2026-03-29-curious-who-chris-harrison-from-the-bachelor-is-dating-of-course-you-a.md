# Curious Who Chris Harrison From 'The Bachelor' Is Dating? Of Course You Are.

Chris Harrison is the guiding hand on The Bachelor, The Bachelorette, Bachelor in Paradise, and The Bachelor Winter Games. You've probably deduced that Harrison is heavily involved in the personal lives of the people who appear on the various shows that he hosts (he's even officiated a bunch of their weddings), but you might not know as much about his personal life, or who he's dated. Here's everything to know about his romantic history, including who he was married to and who he's dating now.

Is Chris Harrison married?

Not anymore. Harrison was married to his high school sweetheart, Gwen, for 18 years. They announced their split in 2012 after being separated for a time. They share two children together.

After his divorce, Harrison began dating, but he told E! News he wasn't looking forward to the single life. "I guess I am a bachelor now, which scares the holy hell out of me—I'm not going to lie—because I haven't been one since the early '90s. And I thought those days were over—and I was happy to have them over!," he said. "I was never one of those married guys that was like, 'Man, I wish I was at the club at 4 a.m. with those guys! I am so happy to be home, I love my life at home."
