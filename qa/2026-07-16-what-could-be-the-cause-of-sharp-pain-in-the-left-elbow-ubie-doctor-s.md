Explanation

Sharp pain in your left elbow can have several causes. One common cause is cubital tunnel syndrome, which occurs when the ulnar nerve near the elbow becomes compressed. This compression can cause sharp or burning pain that may radiate to the forearm or hand. An injury or inflammation to the inner or outer elbow is another possibility. This often results from overuse, which irritates the muscles and tendons attached to the bone. The pain typically worsens with bending or lifting movements. Trauma from a fall or direct impact can also damage the elbow's ligaments, bones, or other structures, leading to sudden, sharp pain. The location of pain, along with your activity history or injury details, can help identify the specific cause. It's important to see a doctor for proper evaluation and treatment recommendations.
