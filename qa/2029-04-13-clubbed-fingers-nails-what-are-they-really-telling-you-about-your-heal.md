Clubbing finger/nails happens when the space between your nail and bone thicken. This thickening leads to your fingertips looking rounded and enlarged like clubs. You may notice subtle changes in the shape of the nails that can happen over the course of weeks. Clubbing is not an actual disease, rather something that happens as a result of several underlying diseases. Since clubbing is associated with a host of different diseases like lung disease or heart disease, take our free AI symptom checker to help identify potential causes and determine if you should consult a medical professional for further discussion.
