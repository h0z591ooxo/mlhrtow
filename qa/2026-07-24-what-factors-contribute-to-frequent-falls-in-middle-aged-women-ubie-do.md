What factors contribute to frequent falls in middle-aged women?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Frequent falls in middle-aged women can be caused by factors like muscle weakness, medication side effects, and balance issues.

Important Next Steps

Not seeing your question? No worries.

Explanation

Falls in middle-aged women can happen due to several reasons. Muscle weakness is a common factor, as it affects balance and stability. Medications, especially those with anticholinergic effects, can also increase the risk of falls by causing dizziness or drowsiness.
