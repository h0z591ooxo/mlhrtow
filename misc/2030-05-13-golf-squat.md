# Golf Squat

Stand holding a 10-pound dumbbell with hand-over-hand grip. Feet should be more than shoulder-width apart. Lower to a squat. Rise, turning to the right while bending your elbows and raising the weight over right shoulder. Hips should face right, with your weight over your right foot and your left heel off the floor. Return to the starting position and repeat to the opposite side. Benefit: In addition to strengthening the glutes, this move increases strength and mobility in the large muscles in the back and shoulders. "The muscles of the shoulders help you control the golf club at the top of your backswing through impact, so it really helps you control the plane of the club and improve consistency on impact," says Roberts.
