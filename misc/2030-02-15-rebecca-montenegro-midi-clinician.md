Rebecca Montenegro, DNP, APRN, WHNP-BC

Depth and breadth in clinical practice: "I’ve been caring for patients since 2010, when I began my career as a medic in the United States Air Force, working in emergency and combat medicine. That foundation taught me how to stay calm, listen closely, and advocate fiercely—skills that continue to shape how I care for women today. After transitioning into civilian healthcare, I worked in labor and delivery from 2019 to 2024, supporting women through some of the most vulnerable and powerful moments of their lives."

The impact of listening: "In labor and delivery, I often served as a patient’s voice—making sure they felt safe, respected, and supported. Some of those experiences led to lifelong connections with patients who’ve told me I played a meaningful role in their birth story. Later, as I began caring for women in midlife, I heard a familiar refrain: ‘thank you for actually listening.’ Too many women feel their menopause concerns are brushed aside, and those moments reinforced how deeply needed this work is."

Collaborative care: "After extensive time spent caring for women, I returned to my alma mater Duke University to complete my Doctorate of Nursing Practice. My approach is simple but intentional: I want my patients to feel seen, heard, cared for, hopeful, and empowered. I take time to understand not just symptoms, but how they affect the patient’s daily life, and what each woman hopes to gain from her care. Together, we build a plan that supports her goals and helps her feel like herself again."
