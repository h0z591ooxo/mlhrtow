Do SARMs Cause Hair Loss?

It is indeed true that some SARMs have been shown to cause hair loss, but what many don’t know is that some SARMs prevent hair loss.

Because each SARM is completely unique, they have different qualities as well as impacting hair loss in different ways. Generally speaking, it all comes down to just how suppressive each SARM is.

SARMs reduce natural testosterone production, with many SARMs suppressing these levels to a point where they can have harmful impacts on the body. Alternatively, some SARMs barely stifle natural testosterone suppression.

When testosterone levels drop, the formation of DHT, a hormone linked to hair loss, also changes. If a SARM suppresses testosterone to a safe level, it might actually help prevent hair thinning and loss.

However, some SARMs like RAD 140 are more likely to cause hair loss, especially if taken in high doses. On the other hand, SARMs such as Ostarine and Ligandrol are less likely to cause hair shedding.

Do SARMs Convert to DHT?

Why are testosterone levels so important when it comes to hair loss? Because testosterone is a significant contributor to the formation of DHT, which subsequently causes hair loss.

So it goes, that if you’re taking a SARM that suppresses testosterone to safe levels, it can mitigate the chance of hair thinning and losing hair.

But do SARMs convert to DHT? The simple answer is no. SARMs, or selective androgen receptor modulators, do not directly convert to DHT (dihydrotestosterone).

Unlike anabolic steroids, SARMs are designed to selectively bind to androgen receptors in muscle and bone tissues, not in the scalp or prostate where DHT is known to have a significant impact.

However, the relationship between SARMs and DHT is a bit more complex. While SARMs themselves don’t convert to DHT, they can indirectly influence its levels.

By suppressing natural testosterone production, SARMs might reduce the overall pool of testosterone available to convert to DHT.

This can sometimes lead to lower DHT levels, which might reduce the risk of hair loss. Yet, this suppression must be carefully managed to avoid negative side effects.

Common Side Effects of SARMS

SARMs, or selective androgen receptor modulators, are popular for their muscle-building benefits. However, they can also have side effects. Here are some common ones to be aware of:

Testosterone Suppression

One of the most common side effects is testosterone suppression. SARMs can lower your body’s natural testosterone production. This can lead to fatigue, mood swings, and a decrease in libido. It’s important to monitor testosterone levels during use.

Liver Toxicity

Some SARMs can be toxic to the liver. This is more common with higher doses and long-term use. Symptoms might include fatigue, nausea, and jaundice. Regular liver function tests are recommended.

Hormonal Imbalances

SARMs can cause hormonal imbalances. This might lead to side effects like acne, hair loss, and mood changes. These imbalances can affect both men and women.

Increased Risk of Heart Problems

There is an increased risk of heart problems when using SARMs. Some users have reported high blood pressure and cholesterol issues. Keeping an eye on cardiovascular health is crucial.
