# Sylvester Stallone Shared a Throwback to the 'Biggest Selfie Ever' With His Action Hero Entourage

Like the rest of us living in a pandemic, Sylvester Stallone misses his buds. In fact, the action movie icon misses his Expendables crew so much that the star just posted a throwback on Instagram to ‘biggest selfie ever,' which features countless iconic action hero actors together.

Now, the "selfie" is more of a selfie in video form — a short clip featuring a buff bunch of your favorite action stars. There’s Antonio Banderas, Mel Gibson, Wesley Snipes, Jason Statham, Harrison Ford and, of course, Arnold Schwarzenegger—plus a bunch more of your Expendables (and action movie) faves.

[Ever' With His Action](https://mimi567.com/1pl0ctim/)

“Look at this group. Look at this. Biggest selfie ever,” Sly says in the video, smiling in the sun with his sunglass-wearing crew.

But don’t worry about the lack of masks or social distancing in the selfie. The video appears to be a behind-the-scenes throwback clip to an Expendables 3 press conference at Cannes in May 2014. You know, the same year when Ellen DeGeneres's 2014 Oscar selfie absolutely owned the internet. The photo serves as obvious inspiration for this action-hero video version.

Ah, 2014. Those were the days—and Stallone agrees.

“The gang like no other,” Sly captioned the photo. “Miss these guys…”
