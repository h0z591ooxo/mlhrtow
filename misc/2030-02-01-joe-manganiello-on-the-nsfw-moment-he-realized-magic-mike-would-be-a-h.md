Manganiello says he thought Magic Mike was poised to fail. Early into production, the film hadn't been picked up by a studio, and so it began as an independent project."We made it for like six million bucks in like, maybe twenty-three days at the most?" Manganiello said. "It might have even been less. Nobody is gonna see this."

But then the clothes started coming off. For one of the dancing numbers, the crew brought in an audience of extras: scores of girls who had no idea what was about to happen."We came out and did that first routine and they were just frozen in their chairs," he said. "The lights go out. The dance is over. We all go backstage and there was this pause for maybe five to ten seconds. And then there was some rustling. And then we just heard this deafening screaming. And it was like, oh shit."

On another occasion, a girl ripped off McConaughey's thong, forcing him to tuck and roll backwards to get away. "The girls were animals," Manganiello said. "Absolute animals."

During opening night, Manganiello said the cast was stampeded by fans. Security was forced to literally hold people back. As the ticket sale numbers began trickling in, the cast was shocked. "[We were] going holy shit! Oh my godddd!”

Since its release, Magic Mike has grossed over $160 million and spawned the massive sequel Magic Mike XXL. Manganiello said the sequel was the movie the cast really wanted to make.

Of course Manganiello and the rest of the cast couldn't ignore all the fun that made the movie something special. "To take that seriously without any form of irony, that’s probably my favorite type of comedy."
