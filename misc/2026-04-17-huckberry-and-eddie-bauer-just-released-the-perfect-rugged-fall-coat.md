As the air chills, you've likely come to terms with the fact that a mere hoodie just isn't cutting it anymore. Yes, fall is certainly here—and winter right behind it. You, my dear sir, need a jacket. Lucky for you, Huckberry teamed up with Eddie Bauer to create an updated version of the latter brand's iconic down bomber jacket—and it's the fall-winter coat you've been waiting for.

The classic Eddie Bauer down jacket gets a bit of a makeover in the brand's collaboration with Huckberry. Not only is the jacket available in an exclusive tan colorway as part of the collaboration, but the jacket also features a water-resistant wax coating for additional protection from the elements.

Huckberry x Eddie Bauer Skyliner Jacket

The Skyliner Jacket also features a soft red flannel lining for additional warmth and style, along with two zippered hand pockets at the hips and interior pockets for additional, secure storage.

The coat is filled with "ultra high-loft 800 fill premium down" which is Responsible Down Certified. This means the feathers come from geese that "have not been subjected to any unnecessary harm," according to global nonprofit Textile Exchange. This means the animals, though still slaughtered for harvesting the down feathers, are not live-plucked or force-fed in the process.

As Huckberry outlines in the jacket's product description and a blog post introducing the jacket, designer Eddie Bauer created the very first down jacket after experiencing a life-threatening case of hypothermia. The jacket was created in 1936, and first patented and sold on the market in 1939. That's some pretty rich history, making this $300 investment buy that much sweeter. Not to mention the coat will last you years upon years, thanks to its sturdy construction.

If it hasn't gone out of style in 82-year history, you can guarantee it will be part of your wardrobe for the long haul.
