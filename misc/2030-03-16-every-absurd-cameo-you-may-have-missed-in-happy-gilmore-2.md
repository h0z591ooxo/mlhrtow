# Every Absurd Cameo You May Have Missed in 'Happy Gilmore 2'

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

HAPPY GILMORE HAS gotten way more star-happy in its sequel, Netflix’s Happy Gilmore 2. The long-awaited follow-up to the legendary 1996 Adam Sandler comedy, which hit the streamer on July 25 to much fanfare and shock over one violent twist, is stuffed with more famous faces than you can likely count. (Hint: Sandler outdoes himself, even as a comedian who especially in his perch at Netflix loves bringing the whole gang out.)

The latest Sandler joint finds us following Happy (Sandler) as he comes out of retirement 30 years after his first Tour Championship win and attempts to reclaim golf glory. There are all your favorites from the OG film, naturally: Julie Bowen as Virginia, the love interest turned wife; Christopher McDonald as the truly reprehensible rival Shooter McGavin; Ben Stiller as Hal L. (whom Sandler obsessives may remember in Hubie Halloween as well), plus Kevin Nealon as a fellow golfer and Dennis Dugan as league commissioner. Robert Smigel also shows up again, though this time as a lawyer rather than an IRS agent.

That would be enough for several movies stuffed with absurdist sports humor. But Sandler decided to stuff some new favorite actors into this one. So many you may need a chart to figure it all out—which is why we put together this handy guide to the extra happy star casting, which sees everyone from Margaret Qualley to Bad Bunny (!) joining the fray.

We won’t spoil Happy’s results on the green, of course, which is the kind of amusement only Sandler, even in his later career period, can provide. Here are the (ridiculously vast) cameos in Happy Gilmore 2 you need to know.

The (Actual) Pro Golfers and Athletes

Happy Gilmore 2 may have as much to do with real golfing as Parks and Recreation faithfully captures local government work, but you have to flex the real deal in this kind of movie. And so Sandler does.

We glimpse a dizzying number of golfers, but here’s a rundown of notable names: Bryson DeChambeau, Rory McIlroy, Brooks Koepka (his wife Jena Sims is an unhinged fan), and Scottie Scheffler play the four golfers who need to defend the PGA tour against Frank Manatee's Maxi League. Xander Schauffele shows up in the clubhouse desperate to get off a "That's What She Said" joke. Justin Thomas, Tony Finau, and Bubba Watson pop in as as themselves/golfers, naturally. Even older golfers, like Jack Nicklaus, Nick Faldo, and Lee Trevino (who appeared in the first movie as well) make appearances. Will Zalatoris, meanwhile, both plays himself—and the grown-up version of Happy Gilmore's original caddy from the first film, following up on a joke people have been making for years. Fun!

John Daly

The legend John Daly shows up for more than just a cameo, in fact, playing himself as Happy's permanent houseguest.

Travis Kelce

There are so many more former and current athletes and figures from the world of sports to list here. But we will say that, yes, Travis Kelce is everywhere again, this time playing a waiter.

Reggie Bush

Retired football star Reggie Bush plays an identity-confused golfer named 8-Ball.

The Music Superstars

An appearance from Puerto Rican music sensation Bad Bunny (as Happy’s new caddie Oscar, no less) would be enough to turn heads. But no, Sandler also gives us Post Malone (in a more understandable role as a DJ/Maxi Golf commentator). And rap god Eminem shows off his foul-mouthed acting chops as the son of Joe Flaherty’s Donald, the heckler in the first film.

Bad Bunny

Bad Bunny is a true standout in the film as Oscar, Happy's new caddy.

Post Malone

Post plays a Maxi League commentator alongside returning Happy Gilmore broadcasters Verne Lundquist (as himself) and Jack Giarraputo (as Jack Beard).

Eminem

The Real Slim Shady himself shows up to play the son of the late great Joe Flaherty's character from the original Happy Gilmore, best known as the "Jackass!" guy who drove his car into the field and injured Happy in the film's final act.

Adam Sandler’s Family

Hey, if Judd Apatow can do it, why not Sandler? The nepo baby craze continues, except here it’s more like a sweet (and massive) family reunion. There’s the comic’s wife Jackie playing a dance instructor, his daughter Sadie playing an FBI asset involved in some Happy-related intrigue. In a particularly sweet move, Sandler’s daughter Sunny plays, well, Happy’s daughter with ambitious dreams of becoming a professional dancer—much like Happy's own dreams of becoming a hockey player (and then a Golfer). Then there’s Sandler’s mom Judy Sandler, brother-in-law Christopher Titone, and mother-in-law Lila Titone playing all kinds of small oddball roles.
