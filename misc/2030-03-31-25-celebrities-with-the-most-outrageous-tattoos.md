# 25 Celebrities With the Most Outrageous Tattoos

Since the Neolithic age, men and women cross culturally have been tattooing their bodies under the guise of religious veneration or cosmetic expression, a process giving corporeal manifestation to presumed hereditary and tribal allegiances. Though historically viewed as the taboo insignia of fringe societal subsets, this list is indicative of a larger cultural shift in the perception of tattooing. In 2012, just 21% of adult Americans had a tattoo, a share that has since ballooned to nearly a third by 2022.

In 2011, the Atlanta rapper debuted his newest and most notable ink - a triple scoop ice cream cone emblazoned with the word “brrr” on his right cheek. A spokesman for the trap pioneer told Rolling Stone the tattoo was a reminder to fans Mane chose to live his life “cool as ice.” So icy, purportedly, you may even say “brrr.”

Steve-O

The American stuntman and entertainer has collected about as many absurd tattoos as he has endured injuries throughout filming the Jackass franchise. Most notably, perhaps, is his self-portrait occupying nearly the entirety of his back.

Presley Gerber

The 23 year-old son of models Cindy Crawford and Rande Gerber raised eyebrows in February 2020 when he showed off his newest ink: a script tattoo of the word “misunderstood” below his right eye. Gerber addressed the ensuing backlash his ink garnered on his Instagram story writing, “Most and a lot of people can get a face lifts, change genders, lip injections, etc and it's offensive to say anything in today's day in age but I get a little face tattoo and now people love to hate me..Hmmmm?” It appears, however, the young model has since had the tattoo removed.

Ben Affleck

In 2019, while filming Netflix’s Top Frontier, paparazzi shots of the actor’s beach scenes surfaced showing him sporting a tattoo seemingly depicting a phoenix rising out of the actor’s buttocks. Affleck originally denied the tattoo’s authenticity in 2016, but would later confirm the tattoo’s legitimacy, telling daytime host Ellen DeGeneres in 2019 the piece “represents something deeply personal to me”.

Amber Rose

The former video vixen had the words “Bash Slash” scrawled across the crown of her forehead sometime in 2020. Rose later revealed the ink was intended as an homage to her children, Sebastian and Slash.

Moby

In 2019, 90s dance music mainstay and animal rights activist Moby had the words “vegan for life” tatted on the right side of his throat by celebrity tattoo artist Kat Von D.

Mike Tyson

Pamela Anderson

As one of the most recognizable pop culture figures from the 1990s, it makes sense that the glamour model would have a tattoo embodying the trendiest ink movement of the time in the form of a barbed wire armband. Perhaps speaking to the tattoo’s anachronistic appearance post Y2K, it appears Anderson has since had the ink removed.

The 27 year-old rapper recently had the words “always tired” tattooed underneath each of his eyes to, ostensibly, give his under-eye bags a more literal expression. When asked about the reception of his rather idiosyncratic taste in tattoos, the White Iverson singer quipped, “I got a face for radio anyways, so why not?”

In 2021, the Grammy award-winning singer had a meme depicting a befuddled Jackie Chan tattooed on the back of his right hand.

Charlie Sheen

Circa 2011, the embattled comedy actor had his notoriously obnoxious catchphrase “winning” tattooed on his wrist in quasi comic sans font.

Rihanna

The multi hyphenate star has a tattoo of Isis, the Egyptian goddess of motherhood and fertility, right below her breastbone in commemoration of her late grandmother, Clara.

Shawn Mendes
