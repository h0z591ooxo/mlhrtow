Dawn Wells, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

Experience in care: "I began my nursing career in 2000 in a small intensive care unit in my hometown. Over the years, I’ve gained experience in women’s surgery, hemodialysis, emergency medicine, and urgent care. Working in fast-paced settings helped me internalize the importance of truly listening and responding thoughtfully to each patient’s needs."

Inspired by women’s midlife health: "As I entered my own perimenopausal years, I became deeply interested in women’s health, especially during midlife. When I discovered Midi, I knew it was the right opportunity to shift my focus from reactive to preventative care."

Listening, first: "A patient once told me how much they appreciated that I took the time to truly listen and validate their concerns. That experience reaffirmed my belief that empathy and understanding are at the heart of effective care—they build trust, strengthen relationships, and lead to better outcomes. I strive for every woman I see to leave feeling heard, respected, and confident in the plan we’ve created together."

Tailored, evidence-based treatment: "Every woman’s midlife experience is unique, and I take a personalized approach to symptom management. My goal is to provide compassionate, evidence-based care that helps women feel empowered and thrive through every stage of life."
