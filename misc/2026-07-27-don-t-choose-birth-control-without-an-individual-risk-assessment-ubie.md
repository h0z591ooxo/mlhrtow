Explanation

Choosing the right birth control method is important because each person is different, and what works well for one person might not be safe or effective for another.

Health Risks and Benefits

Hormonal contraceptives can have different effects based on a person's health history. Some people may have a higher risk of blood clots when using certain hormonal methods.

Non-hormonal options might be better for those who are concerned about these risks or have a history of health issues.

Personal Health Factors

Factors like age, smoking status, and personal or family history of certain health conditions can influence which birth control method is safest.
