# LeBron James Showed Off His New Sneakers and We Think They Look Like Dead Snakes

With all due respect to the King, Cleveland Cavaliers superstar LeBron James might need to rethink his sneaker angle after leaking photos of the upcoming LeBron 15s on his Instagram this weekend.

While some people thought the shoes were rad (including LeBron, who captioned the pic with “I can’t be more excited about these kicks man!!”), the Internet consensus seems to be that the shoes’ “Ghost Colorway” look is closer to dust-covered springboards than a spectral sign of explosiveness. In other words: These things look like dead ribbons of snakeskin. An “Ashes Colorway” has also been teased, and while they take away some of the dry-skin vibes of the Ghost version, they still look closer to an Oreo cookie crust or charcoal briquettes than anything else.

Instagrammers had a mixed response to the shoes, with some commenters on James' post saying "I could live in those" and praising them as "very slick." Others, however, said the shoes looked too similar to the KD 9s put out by Warriors star Kevin Durant earlier this year. One person went as far as to say "Lol eewww."

Perhaps more notable than the scaly look is the sneakers’ conspicuous lack of the classic Nike swoosh, before now a staple of LeBron’s thick-strapped, up-to-the-ankle design. The sole, too, has done away with the NBA star’s traditional solid color block and has replaced it with a pumped-gel look. It’s a bit more “light-up shoes kids wear to the bowling alley” than “best basketball player of his generation,” if you ask us.

The new shoes are set to release October 28 for $185, and Nike promises more details “in the coming weeks.”
