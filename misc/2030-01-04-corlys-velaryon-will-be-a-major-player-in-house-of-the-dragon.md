# Corlys Velaryon Will Be a Major Player in ‘House of the Dragon’

[Velaryon Will Be Major](https://insulin-pathways.hbsnzj.com/)

[Major Player](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/0160422137.html)

If Game of Thrones has taught us anything, it’s to never underestimate anyone close to the throne. For House of the Dragon, viewers should pay close attention to Corlys Velaryon (played by Steve Toussaint)–AKA "The Sea Snake"–the wealthy seafarer who sits on King Viserys I’s council, and husband to Princess Rhaenys Targaryen.

In a brief flashback from Episode 1, Rhaenys is passed over for the throne, and instead Viserys is chosen to lead Westeros. Although she appears to take it gracefully, Fire & Blood, which the series is based on, shows Rhaenys and her husband Corlys aren’t done vying for the throne just yet.

Corlys is the lord of the House of Velaryon. The family rules Driftmark, the largest island in Blackwater Bay. They're also known for their impressive naval army. Throughout history, they’ve been close allies with the Targaryens. In Game of Thrones, the Velaryons are largely in the background, and serve as fierce supporters of Stannis Baratheon, whose brother, King Robert Baratheon, bequeaths Stannis Dragonstone, the castle in Blackwater Bay.

Corlys commands Westeros' largest navy.

[Be Major Player](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/)

Before gaining his seat on Viserys I’s council, Corlys sailed all over Westeros. His exploits were extensive, and he gained his alias The Sea Snake when he built his own ship, aptly named Sea Snake. Eventually he also became knighted and married Rhaenys. Around the same time Rhaenys became pregnant with their first child, King Jaehaerys I announced his new heir would be Baelon (Viserys’ father), passing over Rhaenys for the throne for the first time. Corlys was enraged by this slight, notably leaving the king's council. Although Corlys was not able to be put on the throne (only blood-related Targaryens would be an option), he still yearned for the chance to improve his family’s status.

The second time Rhaenys is not named queen is seen in Episode 1 of House of the Dragon, after Baelon dies. By then, Corlys and Rhaenys also have two children–one girl named Laena and one boy named Laenor. This succession comes to a vote among the various lords of Westeros. Although he uses his money and influence, Corlys isn’t able to have Laenor named heir, and instead the title goes to Viserys.

Without spoiling too much of what’s to come, it’s important to know Corlys wields an impressive influence over the lords of Westeros due to his fighting prowess, wealth, and how close he is to the crown. With two children he can wed to powerful allies and an army at his disposal, he’ll be an enticing supporter on House of the Dragon for Daemon or Rhaenaerys, the two people most likely to be named Westeros’ next ruler.

His daughter, Laena, is also important in House of the Dragon.

[House of the Dragon](https://980187.com/l5v1ayu/)

Laena is another member of Corlys’ family who plays an integral part in the conflict over who Viserys’ heir will be. After Aemma, Viserys’ wife, dies in childbirth, Corlys sees an opportunity for Laena to marry Viserys. Alicent Hightower though, Rhaenaerys’ best friend, gains Viserys’ trust quickly and is ultimately the one he chooses to marry.

Still, that’s not the end of Corlys’ goal to wed her to a powerful person. Laena will show up again in House of the Dragon, and will gain the favor of another major player in the fight for the crown.
