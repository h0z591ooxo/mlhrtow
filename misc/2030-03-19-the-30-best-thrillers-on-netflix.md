# The 30 Best Thrillers on Netflix

Sometimes you just want to curl up on the couch, turn on your TV, and relax by watching Marie Kondo tell strangers to throw away most of their earthly possessions (yeah, we do it too). Other times, you're in the mood for some mindless reality TV, or a gripping war flick. And sometimes you want to watch something that will grab you, unsettle you, and keep your eyes glued to the screen even if you want to look away.

Of course, Netflix is perfect for finding movies that will do just that, and the streaming giant's thriller selection is unmatched. From the futuristic to the paranormal to the psychological, we can guarantee that there's a movie for you—these 30 thrillers all share a relentless intensity and a willingness to look at the darker side of life and humanity. Watch, and be thrilled.

Double Jeopardy

Ashley Judd plays a woman framed for her husband's murder. When she discovers that he's still alive, she decides to kill him for real. Tommy Lee Jones plays her parole officer.

Snowpiercer

While Bong Joon-ho shot to fame after his film Parasite won Best Picture at the 2020 Oscars, Snowpiercer was actually shot back in 2013. The film takes place on a train that houses the last of humanity after an attempt to stop global warming accidentally created a new Ice Age.

However, passengers on the train are now separated by income, and one man (Chris Evans) decides to lead a revolution in order to get better conditions for those in lower-class. The movie also stars Song Kang-ho, Tilda Swinton, Jamie Bell, Octavia Spencer, Go Ah-sung, John Hurt, and Ed Harris.

Bird Box

Bird Box was the highlight of Thanksgiving 2018, but if you haven't seen the movie yet, here's a brief breakdown—in a post-apocalyptic world, survivors have to cover their eyes when they outside or they'll be compelled to kill themselves by an unknown entity. Malorie Hayes (Sandra Bullock) ends up in a house with some other survivors, and the rest of the movie follows her as she tries to navigate this new world with her two children.

Inside Man

This movie has some big names behind it: Spike Lee is the director, while the cast includes Denzel Washington, Chiwetel Ejiofor, Jodie Foster, Willem Dafoe, Clive Owen, and Christopher Plummer.

The film follows the story of an elaborate Wall Street bank over 24 hours, and you get to see all the action from the point of view of the bank robbers, hostage negotiator, and bank's founder.

El Camino: A Breaking Bad Story

[Thrillers on](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/)

Aaron Paul reprised his award-winning role as Jesse Pinkman in El Camino, the official Breaking Bad epilogue. Expect the slow burn that also defined BB, but when the action ramps up, it goes to new heights. The actors that played fan favorites like Skinny Pete, Badger, and Mike Ehrmantraut all returned for El Camino, and there's also one reappearance that we won't spoil.

Uncut Gems

Adam Sandler's star turn as jeweler and gambling addict Howard Ratner gained him critical acclaim, and Uncut Gems was also listed on a list of the top 10 movies of 2019. Lakeith Stanfield, Idina Menzel, Julia Fox, Kevin Garnett, and Eric Bogosian fill out the cast.

Sleepless

Sleepless stars Jamie Foxx as Vincent Downs, a corrupt Las Vegas police officer that steals a shipment of a mobster's cocaine. The mobsters then retaliate by kidnapping his son, and Downs has to race against time to get him back.

Extraction

This action thriller was a Netflix fan favorite when it premiered back in April, and it follows former soldier turned mercenary Tyler Rake (played by Chris Hemsworth), after he's hired to rescue an Indian drug lord's kidnapped son in Bangladesh.

Apostle

Visionary action director Gareth Evans (The Raid: Redemption) switches gears for this period piece set in 1905, about a disillusioned Christian missionary (a twitchy Dan Stevens) who heads to a Welsh island to find his missing sister. There, he infiltrates a religious cult that’s seized control of the isle, and discovers that they’re hiding some pretty dark secrets (although really, has there ever been a secret cult in a movie that wasn’t up to no good?). It’s a bit deflating that the film’s conclusion leaves behind the exploration of life in a cult in favor of a more generic, supernatural-flavored confrontation between hero and villain, but at least it provides plenty of shocks and gripping set pieces along the way.

Sweetheart

Kiersey Clemons stars as women shipwrecked on a remote island, but she soon learns that she's not as alone on the island as she once thought. The thrilling and visually stunning movie was shot in the Mamanuca Islands, Fiji—aka where Survivor films its seasons.

I Don't Feel at Home in This World Anymore

Finally, a movie hero we can all relate to: Ruth (Melanie Lynskey) is a nursing assistant who’s sick and tired of living in a world where assholes ruin everything and nobody has the courage to stop them. When her laptop is stolen during a home burglary, she decides she’s had enough and takes matters into her own hands, tracking down and confronting the thieves with the help of her weirdo, karate-obsessed neighbor (Elijah Wood). So begins a darkly hilarious odyssey into a very low-rent criminal underworld, with Ruth acting as a righteously pissed-off avenger who’s willing to confront society’s worst jerks and stand up for basic human decency.

Green Room

Saulnier’s next film, 2015’s Green Room, is one of the best “trapped in a single location” thrillers ever made. A down-on-their-luck punk band make the mistake of playing a bar in rural Oregon that doubles as a home base for a neo-Nazi gang. When they accidentally witness a murder there, the neo-Nazis (led by Patrick Stewart!) hold them hostage in the backstage green room, forcing the group to fight their way out. The film does an outstanding job of continually ramping up the tension, as the band’s attempts to outsmart their captors eventually turn into a battle to the death.

Salt

Angelina Jolie stunned in this 2010 film about a woman named Evelyn Salt that is accused of being a Russian sleeper agent and then goes on the run to try and clear her name. Fun fact - the role was originally written with a male protagonist in mind, with Tom Cruise secured as the lead. Once Jolie came on board, the script was rewritten.

Blue Ruin

All four feature films of director Jeremy Saulnier, more or less the reigning champ of low-budget thrillers, are currently available on Netflix. We recommend starting off with Blue Ruin, his 2013 breakout film, which centers on a vagrant named Dwight (Macon Blair) as he seeks vengeance for the murder of his parents after the killer is released from jail early. Saulnier takes what initially seems like a straightforward revenge thriller and twists it into an almost Kafkaesque tragicomedy about the impossibility of finding satisfaction through violence.

Hold the Dark

Like many promising filmmakers these days, Saulnier was only able to jump from low-budget endeavors to something larger in scope when Netflix offered to bankroll his next film (since, unfortunately, the mid-budget tier basically doesn’t exist anymore at most Hollywood studios). Jeffrey Wright stars as a renowned naturalist who’s summoned to a small village in Alaska to investigate a series of child disappearances that may be the result of wolf attacks. As is usually the case with Saulnier, that basic setup is just the tip of the iceberg for a story about the dark and animalistic impulses lying at the heart of humanity.

The Ghost Who Walks

A St. Louis area man has provided information to authorities in exchange for an early release from prison, but he now has to try and stay one step ahead of the vengeful gangsters that he betrayed.

1922

[Best Thrillers](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/)

This movie is based on the Stephen King novel of the same name, so you know things are going to get creepy. 1922 follows a disgruntled husband (Thomas Jane) that decides to kill his wife in order to halt the sale of their land. However, her murder leads to many more problems for the man.

The Invitation

Will's ex-wife Eden has invited him to a dinner party, and he brings his new girlfriend Kira along. While that already sounds like a recipe for disaster, the former couple had previously lost their child in an accident, and Will spends much of the evening reliving memories of his late son. And while he spots some suspicious behavior from some of the guests during the night, Will simply chalks it up to his grief—but it turns out the real reason for his invitation that night is much more sinister than he ever could have realized.

Lila & Eve

Lila & Eve stars Viola Davis and Jennifer Lopez as two grieving mothers whose children were gunned down in a drive-by, so they team up to avenge their deaths. However, there's a twist in the plot that no one will see coming.

2012

2012 is a thriller of the post-apocalyptic variety, and the film follows the story of several different people as they try to survive as the world seemingly comes to an end in the year 2012. The ensemble cast includes John Cusack, Chiwetel Ejiofor, Thandie Newton, Danny Glover, Amanda Peet, Oliver Platt, and Woody Harrelson.
