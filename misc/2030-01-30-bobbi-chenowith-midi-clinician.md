Bobbi Chenowith, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

Learning together: “I’ve been in nursing since 2007, with the last 11 focused on women’s health as a certified Family Nurse Practitioner. I chose this path because it allows me to have a direct impact on patients’ lives through holistic care and patient education. Helping women understand their health and make informed decisions has always been my top priority.”

A personal connection: “During my years as an OB/GYN Nurse Practitioner, I primarily focused on the childbearing years. It wasn’t until I personally experienced perimenopause that I truly understood the complexity of midlife care. The added perspective reshaped my approach and fueled my passion for supporting women through this transformative stage of life.”

Privileged to care: “What I love most about midlife care is helping women feel seen and supported. At Midi, I get to provide personalized, evidence-based care that truly transforms my patients. It’s such an honor to see them regain their sense of self and live their lives to the fullest.”
