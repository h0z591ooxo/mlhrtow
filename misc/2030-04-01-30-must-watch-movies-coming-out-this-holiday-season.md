Thanksgiving

Want a little horror for the holidays? Check out Eli Roth's Thanksgiving, where an axe murderer descends upon Black Friday shoppers in Plymouth, Massachusetts.

EXmas

Leighton Meester and Robbie Amell star in EXmas. It follows a guy who returns home for the holidays to find his family is already celebrating...with his ex.

The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes

It may not be holiday-themed, but The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes is a gripping YA drama that'll keep you glued to your seat this holiday season. A prequel to the famed trilogy, this film details a much earlier version of the Hunger Games, tracking the rise of President Snow, who was back then only a determined teen.

Dashing Through the Snow

This Hulu original stars Lil Rel Howery and Ludacris. A social worker no longer believes in the magic of Christmas, so his wife convinces him to bring his daughter to work, where the two begin a magical adventure.

May December

Another non-holiday entry, May December is both interesting and heartbreaking. For an upcoming role, an actress decides to get to close to her subject: a teacher who seduced her student years ago and is now married to him with children.

Genie

Melissa McCarthy stars as a genie who helps a man to win his family life after his workaholic lifestyle drives them away.

Family Switch

Fans of Freaky Friday may like Family Switch, which stars Jennifer Garner and Ed Helms as parents who end up switched with their daughter and son, respectively, and must find a way back into their bodies before Christmas.

It's a Wonderful Knife

Love It's a Wonderful Life, but want a bit more of an adult version? Try out It's a Wonderful Knife. The slasher follows a woman who saved her town from a psychotic killer the year before, but feels like her life has only gone downhill. When she wishes she'd never been born, however, things get very, very violent.

Silent Night

Silent Night follows a father out for revenge after he sees his son killed due to warring gangs. The film comes from director John Woo, who's well-known for his gripping and intense action films.

Candy Cane Lane

Eddie Murphy, Tracee Ellis Ross and more star in this Christmas comedy about a father who accidentally signs a Faustian bargain with an evil elf. He has to recruit the rest of his family to save his life and the holiday season.

Rescuing Christmas
