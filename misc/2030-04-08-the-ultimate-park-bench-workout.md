# The Ultimate Park Bench Workout

If you like working up a sweat outdoors, you’ll love this workout from Men’s Health Fitness Director B.J. Gaddour, creator of THE 21-DAY METASHRED. It’s a total-body metabolic routine you can do al fresco with the help of a park bench—because the only thing more satisfying than a sweat-drenched forehead is the feeling of a cool breeze on it.

Watch the video above to see how to do the exercises, and for easy reference, here’s the breakdown:

Perform 10 reps per side of each of the following exercises, resting only when necessary. That’s 1 round. Do 5 to 10 rounds.

1. Single-leg hip thrusts 2. Bulgarian split squats 3. Feet-elevated pushups 4. Alternating stepup jumps
