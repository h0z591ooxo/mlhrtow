SARMs and Testosterone

SARMs are selective androgen receptor modulators. As such, they are far more specific in their actions than steroids.

We could say that steroids take a shotgun approach to alter your hormones to build muscle whereas SARMs take a sniper approach.

There are actually two different types of SARMs – steroidal and non-steroidal. The type of steroids that we are interested in for body transformation and strength development are nonsteroidal SARMS.

Many SARMs produce muscle and strength-building effects by simulating the male growth hormone testosterone. This is great for men but not so good for women as it leads to the virilization that is referred to in the introduction.

There are, however, a number of SARMs that do not produce a testosterone-boosting effect that are both safe and effective for women to use.

What happens if a woman takes SARMs?

If a woman takes a SARM that enhances natural testosterone production, she may develop such unwanted side effects as facial hair growth, voice deepening, and clitoris enlargement. However, by sticking to the relatively safe SARMs that we have focused on in this article, she will not have those virilization-related side effects.

However, there is still the possibility that she may have such side effects as nausea, headaches, and bloating.

If a woman takes SARMs in conjunction with smart resistance training and proper nutrition she can reach her body transformation goals faster as well as make significant strength gains.

Do SARMs affect estrogen?

SARMs are non-aromatizing, meaning that it will convert to estrogen in the body. They can, however, lower the body’s natural production of estrogen after 2-3 weeks of use. You can counter this effect by taking a SERM (selective estrogen receptor modulator) along with your SARM cycle.

Do SARMs have side effects?

Yes, there are some potential side effects of taking SARMs. The biggest side effect is testosterone suppression, which is why, as described above, women are strongly advised to stay away from any SARMs that have this effect. Other side effects of SARMs may include hair loss, nausea, diarrhea, bloating, lethargy, back pain, and mood swings.
