How We Measured Beauty: Methodology & Sources

To explore how beauty is perceived across the U.S., we analyzed 10–20 well-known public figures from each state—men and women from entertainment, sports, politics, and culture. Each face was scored in five key areas:

Facial Harmony – balance and proportion of features

Skin Quality – tone, clarity, and visible health

Smile & Expression – warmth, openness, and emotional presence

Grooming & Style – personal presentation and polish

Charisma – overall presence, confidence, and that “spark”

Scores were generated using AI-driven image analysis tools:

Microsoft Azure Computer Vision for facial landmark detection

Face++ API for structural analysis and expression scoring

OpenAI GPT-4o for interpreting patterns and drawing insights

Each state’s final score was calculated by averaging male and female results, then combining them into a single composite ranking. Only publicly available images were used—no private or user-submitted content was included.

To add clinical context, all AI-generated scores were reviewed using aesthetic frameworks from:

The Aesthetic Society (ASAPS)

The International Society of Aesthetic Plastic Surgery (ISAPS)

Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology
