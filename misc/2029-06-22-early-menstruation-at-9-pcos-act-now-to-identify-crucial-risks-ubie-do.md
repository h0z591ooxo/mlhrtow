What factors could cause early menstruation at age 9 in someone with PCOS?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Early menstruation at age 9 in girls with PCOS is often linked to several key factors: elevated body weight (especially obesity), genetic predisposition, and psychosocial influences such as family stress or an unstable home environment. Hormonal imbalances characteristic of PCOS—like elevated androgens and insulin resistance—can also accelerate puberty onset. Understanding these triggers is critical because early menarche in PCOS may increase long-term risks for metabolic and reproductive health issues.
