# Hey, Look, Zack Got Jacked: Inside the Fitness Evolution of Mark-Paul Gosselaar

[Mark-Paul Gosselaar](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/blog/9218456808.html)

Time Out! Put down your oversized cell phone and cancel your plans at The Max, because we’ve got to talk about Mark-Paul Gosselaar.

Gosselaar (heretofore known as MPG) is probably most famous for portraying the adorable teen psychopath Zack Morris on the beloved sitcom Saved by the Bell. Since then, he’s built a nice little career for himself, starring in NYPD Blue, Franklin and Bash, and more than a few TV holiday specials. He’s also been getting jacked AF, prompting us to say, "Franklin & Bash? More like Franklin & Bench!" (NB: No one has actually said that.)

But don’t just take our word for it. We’ve got photographic proof of MPG’s evolution, as well as a workout from one of his CrossFit coaches so you can get your sweat on just like your favorite gigantic cell phone owner.

Exhibit A:

MPG’s shirtless pinup graced countless teens’ bedroom walls during the height of Saved By the Bell mania. But although his bottle blonde locks were perfection, the actor still had a bit of baby fat, preventing us from seeing the emerging swole icon within. After the show ended, he took dramatic roles on NYPD Blue and TNT's Franklin & Bash.

While MPG was initially reluctant to show skin, he showed off his physique during the show’s third season in 2013. His secret to a killer torso? It could have been CrossFit, which the actor first mentioned that year, but MPG also credited hardcore home workout P90X with Breckin Meyer for keeping him in fighting shape. Exhibit B:

By 2015, Gosselaar was apparently a CrossFit devotee. (Here's why fitness devotees love the workout so much). Ryan Webb, the owner and coach at CrossFit 228 in Gulfport, Mississippi, said Gosselaar stood out when he dropped by the gym to train while filming the action thriller Precious Cargo. "The dude’s an advanced athlete," Webb told Men's Health. "He’s definitely ahead of the pack.”

That February, the actor announced that he’d be entering the Crossfit Horsepower Competition with partner Ben Ceccarelli, posting a photo on Instagram of the two doing an upside-down nude workout. (Their team name? Appropriately, “Cocky”.)

Later that year, MPG provided further evidence of his transition from preppy to jock by recreating an iconic 1989 photo of him and former Saved by the Bell co-star Mario Lopez, this time with actor Tone Bell standing in as Slater. Did his guns put Lopez's biceps to shame? Perhaps not. Regardless, MPG looked pretty damn fit.

Exhibit C:

In 2016, Gosselaar starred as a catcher in Fox’s Pitch. The 42-year-old whipped himself into killer shape for the show’s high-energy baseball scenes, training with veteran players to ensure maximum authenticity. Gosselaar also went full-on keto, forcing himself to eat more than 3,000 calories a day to beef up for the role. (Want to do keto right? Here’s how.).

Pitch was cancelled after one season, but MPG’s hard work definitely paid off. The cast stripped down for the show’s promos and the father of four, who posed wearing nothing but knee pads and a look of grim determination, showed us that dadbod is nothing but a state of mind.
