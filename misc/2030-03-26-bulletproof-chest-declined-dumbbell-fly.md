# Bulletproof Chest: Declined Dumbbell Fly

Note: To see video clip, please click on the link under related media on the right.

[Chest Declined Dumbbell](https://suppliers.pipeprospect.com/tag/best-peptide-manufacturers-china/)

A pair of dumbbells and a declined bench

For this dumbbell exercise, lie on your back on a decline bench with your knees bent and your feet tucked under the pads at the end of the bench. Grasp two dumbbells, palms in, and hold them at arm's length straight above you.

Notes: If your gym doesn't have a decline bench, use an elevated sit-up bench.

Slowly lower your arms out to your sides, keeping elbows slightly bent.

Notes: Stop when you feel a comfortable stretch across your shoulders.

Pause, then reverse the motion.
