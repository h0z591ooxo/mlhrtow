Megan Bishoff, MSN, APRN, CNM, WHNP-BC

A full-circle path to women’s health: "I’ve been in healthcare since 2009, and since 2020 have worked in women’s health and midwifery. My early career included time in the ER and dermatology, but my own experience during my third pregnancy inspired me to focus on women’s health. I realized how much I loved caring for women through every stage of life, and how needed that care was in my community."

Listening first: "I’ve had the privilege of supporting women through everything from pregnancy and birth to menopause and beyond. What I love most is simply listening, meeting women where they are and helping them feel informed and confident in their decisions. Many of my patients are surprised to learn how much power they have in shaping their own treatment plan, and watching that sense of empowerment grow is incredibly rewarding."

Empowerment through connection: "My care philosophy is rooted in shared decision-making and treating patients as whole people. I want every woman I meet to feel truly heard, respected, and supported—never just like a number."
