# The Rules: Grooming

There's a beast inside most of us. Our inner Sasquatch is hairy and unkempt, doesn't always smell like lemon verbena, and favors shirtless-at-Shea Stadium style. Impatient with the rigors of good grooming, he sometimes stands between us and all the goodies that come with looking great. Sedate the beast with some self-talk about the greater guy glories of maintenance. It is, after all, an eye for detail that makes factories hum, engines thrum, and women . . . uh . . . willing. Remind yourself that the beast, though free, is alone in the woods, while the civilized man, careful about his ministrations, has a warm home and boon companions. Just follow these pay-attention grooming rules and reap the harvest of looking sharp.
