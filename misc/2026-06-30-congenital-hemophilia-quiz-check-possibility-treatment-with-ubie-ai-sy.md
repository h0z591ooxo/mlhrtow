What is Congenital Hemophilia?

Congenital hemophilia is a genetic bleeding disorder resulting in insufficient blood clotting due to a deficiency of clotting factors.

Treatment of Congenital Hemophilia

Congenital hemophilia treatment aims to prevent and control bleeding episodes through factor replacement therapy, which involves administering the missing clotting factors. Prophylactic treatment may also be recommended for severe cases.
