# Jensen Ackles Is Bearded and Buff in New Shirtless Photo Ahead of His 'The Boys' Debut

Jensen Ackles hasn't even made his entrance in the third season of The Boys on Amazon Prime Video yet, but he's already causing a stir online. In anticipation of the release on June 3, Ackles just posted some shirtless throwback photos from a year ago when he was filming his episodes.

"That's right stud!!!" wrote Seal Team actor Max Theriot, while This Is Us star Justin Hartley simply commented: "Jacked!" Ackles will play Soldier Boy in the new season of The Boys.
