What are the health consequences of chronically disturbed sleep?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Chronically disturbed sleep is linked to serious health risks, including a higher likelihood of developing chronic diseases such as heart disease, diabetes, and obesity. Poor sleep also weakens the immune system, making the body more vulnerable to infections and slower to recover from illness. In addition, ongoing sleep disruption is strongly associated with mental health concerns like anxiety, depression, and cognitive decline.
