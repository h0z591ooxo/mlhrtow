Treating Headaches and Migraines at Home

Unless there is another underlying condition, headaches and migraines generally go away on their own within a day. Properly treating headaches and migraines at home can help accelerate your recovery and help you feel back to normal as quickly as possible.

Over-the-counter medication: There are many kinds of over-the-counter painkillers that can bring relief from headaches. Some types of medications contain caffeine, an ingredient which may help improve symptoms in some people.

Drink some caffeine: Caffeine has vasoconstrictive properties that can help restrict the dilated blood vessels that typically come with headaches. A caffeinated beverage such as tea or coffee may help some people and trigger headaches in others, so use your best judgment as to whether this remedy is right for you.

Eat some food: Sometimes, the cause of your headache is related to not eating enough. Try having a snack and see if eating relieves your symptoms.

Sleep: Sleep is truly the best medicine. Even if you only have time for a nap, some shut-eye can help reduce your symptoms and help relieve your pounding head – especially for migraines.

Hydration: When you are dehydrated, the cells in your body do not have adequate hydration to function normally. Cells shrink and tissues contract – including the tissues in your brain, which can contribute to worsened symptoms.

Spend some time in a dark and quiet room without any electronic devices: Lights can be painful for people with migraines. Taking some quiet time away from the stimulus of light can help ease symptoms.
