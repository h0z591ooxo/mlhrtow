Explanation

Viruses can survive for hours to days on common surfaces—doorknobs, countertops, toilets, electronics and more. Safely and effectively disinfecting these areas reduces your risk of exposure to pathogens, including Hantavirus, influenza, coronaviruses and others. This guide explains which products work best, how to use them correctly, and answers the key SEO topic: What kills Hantavirus on surfaces.

Understanding Viruses on Surfaces

Viruses aren't living organisms, but they can cling to surfaces and remain infectious until they're inactivated by:

Heat

Ultraviolet light

Chemical disinfectants

Risk factors depend on:

Virus type (enveloped vs. non-enveloped)

Surface material (hard vs. porous)

Temperature and humidity

Enveloped viruses (like Hantavirus, influenza and coronaviruses) are generally easier to kill with common disinfectants than non-enveloped viruses (like norovirus).

Hantavirus at a Glance

Hantaviruses are carried by rodents (deer mice, cotton rats, etc.).

Infection occurs via inhalation of aerosolized rodent droppings, urine or saliva.

Hantavirus Pulmonary Syndrome (HPS) can be severe or life-threatening.

Environmental persistence: Studies show Hantavirus can survive for days in cool, moist conditions.

Below is a list of common, EPA-approved disinfectants effective against enveloped viruses, including Hantavirus:

1. Sodium Hypochlorite (Bleach)

Example: Mix 1 part bleach to 49 parts water for 0.1% solution (1,000 ppm).

Contact time: 1–5 minutes.

Surfaces: Hard, non-porous (tiles, countertops, plastic).

Wear gloves and eye protection.

Ensure good ventilation.

When cleaning spaces with potential rodent contamination:

Ventilation: Open windows and doors for at least 30 minutes before entering.

Protective gear: Wear gloves, an N95 respirator (or better) and eye protection.

Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings—spray them with bleach solution first.

Spray the entire area with disinfectant, let it soak, then carefully wipe or blot.

Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
