# Style Editors Agree: This $600 Suit Looks Like a Million Bucks

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

HERE IS SOMETHING more guys should know: Great-looking suits do not have to be expensive. In fact, you don’t have to go to Savile Row to get a jacket that fits great and feels even better. The best affordable suits for men are formalwear made easy. Just take it to a tailor for a perfect fit, then combine it with a dress shirt and oxford shoes, and you’ll be ready for wedding season or that big job interview.

These days, the best affordable suits come in every fabric and fit imaginable. Want an on-trend boxy jacket and relaxed pants in a breezy chino? You got it. Need a do-it-all navy wool two piece that can handle all four seasons! Done. Looking to expand your collection with a snazzy glen plaid number that can break up into a blazer or dress pant? It’s right here. Best of all, every suit on this list comes in at $800 or less. If you didn't think you needed a new suit before, you probably do now.
