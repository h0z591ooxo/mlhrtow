# Alex Trebek Sent a Sweet Response to a Grandmother With Pancreatic Cancer

Be prepared to admire Alex Trebek even more. The Jeopardy! host, who was diagnosed with advanced pancreatic cancer in March, sent an encouraging hand-written letter to a fan who was also recently diagnosed with pancreatic cancer.

In a Reddit thread, one user shared that their grandmother, a huge Trebek fan, was diagnosed with pancreatic cancer last month. So, the Redditor asked the TV star to send their grandma well wishes.

[Trebek Sent](https://copperglow.dns0663.com/blog/2830875463.html)

"My grandma was diagnosed with pancreatic cancer last month," the post reads. "Her idol, Alex Trebek, was also recently diagnosed with pancreatic cancer. I wrote to Mr. Trebek asking for some words of encouragement and well wishes. To my complete surprise -- he responded! Thank you for taking the time, Mr. Trebek!"

Trebek did not disappoint and sent a photo with a very sweet note that reads, "Karen, let's you and I decide that we both are going to be cancer survivors. Stay positive. All the best! Alex Trebek."
