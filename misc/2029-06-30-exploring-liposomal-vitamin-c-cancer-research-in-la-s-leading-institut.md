Understanding Liposomal Vitamin C

Liposomal Vitamin C is a form of Vitamin C that employs liposomal technology, which involves encapsulating Vitamin C within lipid bilayers.1 This technology creates microscopic liposomes that protect the Vitamin C, enhance its stability, and promote targeted delivery.

This encapsulation improves the absorption and delivery of Vitamin C to cells throughout the body. The liposomes can penetrate cell membranes more efficiently, allowing for improved absorption and increased bioavailability of Vitamin C.

Liposomes protect the Vitamin C from degradation and allow for higher concentrations to be achieved in target tissues. This enhanced bioavailability has sparked interest in exploring its potential application in cancer treatment and its ability to support the immune system. As such, this advanced delivery system has the potential to optimize the therapeutic efficacy of Vitamin C in cancer treatment.
