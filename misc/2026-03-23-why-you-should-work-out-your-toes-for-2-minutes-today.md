Imagine living your life with your hands stuffed into super-padded, rigid gloves. You would barely be able to wiggle your fingers.

The concept may seem ridiculous, but that’s exactly what you do with our feet on a daily basis, says Men’s Health Fitness Director BJ Gaddour.

When you jam your feet into shoes, your toes can’t splay right. Over time, this causes the muscles, tendons, and ligaments in your feet to become extremely tight and weak, he says.

Immobile toes and arches can mess up the mechanics of your ankles and knees and can hinder your form when you lunge, jump, or run, explains Gaddour. You not only decrease your gains at the gym, but also put yourself at serious risk of joint injury.

Bring back the mobility and strength in your feet with this toe extension drill. Gaddour recommends spending just 2 to 5 minutes on each foot before or after your workout.
