Emily Odenwald, MSN, APRN, WHNP-BC

Finding my purpose in care: “I’ve been a Women’s Health Nurse Practitioner since 2018, but my journey began in the surgical ICU years prior. My passion for women’s care led me through roles in family planning, minimally invasive surgery, sexual medicine, and menopause care. I have have deep experience in trauma-informed care, which is essential as so many women carry past experiences that shape how they move through the healthcare system.”

Why trust matters: “My first gynecology visit left me feeling judged and unsafe, and I’ve never forgotten that. It’s shaped how I connect with my patients: I want every woman I care for to feel like she’s in charge, and that I’m here to support her, not scrutinize her. I work hard to build a space where you feel truly heard, respected, and empowered to take the lead in your health journey.”

Why I love Midi: “I joined Midi because we’re on the same page about what women’s healthcare should be: collaborative, compassionate, and centered on you. If it takes thinking outside the box or involving other experts to reach your goals, I’m all in. The best part? I get to witness women reclaim their health and well-being every day, and that never gets old.”
