# 39 Celebrities Who Had Kids Later in Life

Whether they were caught up living the bachelor life or too focused on their career to settle down, these celebrities put off daddy duty until their 40s. From movie stars to politicians, we're breaking down the famous fathers who welcomed their first child in their fourth decade and beyond.

George Clooney, 56

After years as Hollywood's most eligible bachelor, George Clooney stepped into a new role as family man. After his wedding to lawyer Amal Alamuddin in 2014, the couple welcomed twins Ella and Alexander in 2017 when the actor was 56.

Jay-Z, 43

Jay-Z was already in his mid-40s when he welcomed his first child, daughter Blue Ivy, with Beyoncé in 2012. Five years later, the rapper became a dad twice over with the birth of their twins, Rumi and Sir, in 2017.

John Stamos, 54

Always an uncle (Jesse) and never a dad, it wasn't until John Stamos turned 54 that his fatherhood journey began. He welcomed his son, Billy Stamos, with wife Caitlin McHugh in 2018.

Richard Gere, 50

Richard Gere was 50 when he and ex-wife, Carey Lowell, welcomed his first child in 2000. At the age of 70, Gere became a father once again when his second wife, Alejandra Silva, gave birth to their son Alexander in 2019.

Cary Grant, 62

It wasn't until the Hollywood star's fourth marriage to Dyan Cannon that Cary Grant became a father. In 1966, the couple welcomed Jennifer Grant, when the actor was 62 years old.

Andy Cohen, 50

Brad Pitt, 43
