Explanation

Chronic Lyme disease is a condition that some people believe persists even after standard treatment. However, many treatments marketed for it are unproven and not backed by science.

Here are some unproven treatments:

Long-term Antibiotics: Some clinics offer long-term antibiotic therapy, but this is not supported by scientific evidence and can be harmful.

Herbal Supplements: Various herbal products are marketed for Lyme disease, but their effectiveness and safety are not proven.

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy: This involves breathing pure oxygen in a pressurized room, but it is not shown to be effective for Lyme disease.
