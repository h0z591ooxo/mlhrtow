Are hypnic jerks the same as sleep myoclonus?

Hypnic jerks and sleep myoclonus are related but distinct sleep-related movement phenomena. Hypnic jerks (also called sleep starts) are brief, involuntary muscle twitches that occur specifically as you're falling asleep and are considered a normal, common experience. Sleep myoclonus is a broader category that includes various types of sudden muscle movements during sleep, of which hypnic jerks are just one type. Other forms of sleep myoclonus can occur throughout the night and may sometimes indicate an underlying condition.
