# Crush Your Holiday Fitness With These 7-Minute Workouts

WE HAVE REACHED peak holiday time, and you might have missed a workout or two. That's okay. Making time to train during all of the challenges that spring up at the end of the year can be especially tough—especially if you're trying to stick to the same old schedule you follow during the rest of the year.

[These Minute Workouts](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/)

So instead of skipping your strength training, just shorten your sessions. Instead of taking hours in the weight room, these quick 7-minute workouts can serve as a solid substitution while helping to maintain a solid baseline of fitness when you're busy.

Thanks to these two workouts, you don’t need a crushing training plan to maintain your gains during the hustle and bustle of the season. The first helps you build muscle, the second hones cardio—and neither will keep you from holiday fun.

Directions: Take on 1 of these workouts 6 days a week. Start each workout with 1 minute of the warmup move. Rest at least 1 day each week.

Warmup

Inchworm to Scorpion

Start standing. Lean forward and place your hands on the floor, bending your knees just slightly. Walk your hands forward until you’re in pushup position, then lower your chest to the floor. Lift your right leg and reach it over to the left, touching your right foot to the floor. Return to pushup position and repeat on the other side. Reverse the movements to stand up. That’s 1 rep; do reps for 60 seconds.

Workouts

Dumbbell Workout

Do 2 rounds of the following circuit. Rest 60 seconds between rounds. Use medium-weight dumbbells.

Renegade Row

Start in pushup position, hands on dumbbells, feet spread slightly, abs and glutes tight. Bend at the elbows and shoulders, lowering into a pushup, then press back up. As you do this, lift the right dumbbell and row it to the right side of your rib cage. Return to pushup position and repeat on the other side. That’s 1 rep; do reps for 60 seconds.

Mixed-Rack Squat

Stand holding a dumbbell in your left hand at your shoulder and a dumbbell in your right hand at your hip, abs and glutes tight, feet about shoulder-width apart. Push your butt back and bend your knees, lowering your torso until your thighs are parallel to the floor. Stand back up. That’s 1 rep; do reps for 20 seconds on one side, then 20 seconds onthe other.

Dumbbell Swing

Stand about 2 feet behind a single dumbbell, feet shoulder-width apart. Push your butt back, grabbing one end of the dumbbell with both hands. Tighten your abs. This is the start. Now explosively hike the dumbbell between your legs. Stand tall and squeeze your glutes; this will propel the dumbbell forward. Let the dumbbell’s momentum take it back between your legs as you push your butt back, then explosively stand and squeeze your glutes, driving it forward. Repeat this pattern for 20 seconds.

Bodyweight Workout

T-Pushup

Start in pushup position, hands slightly wider than shoulder width, abs and glutes tight. Bend at the elbows and shoulders, lowering your chest to within an inch of the floor. Press back up, then lift your right hand off the floor. Reach it toward the ceiling, turning your chest to the right and squeezing your shoulder blades as you do. That’s 1 rep; do 3 on each side.

Modified V-Up

Lie on your back, arms and legs outstretched. Tighten your abs, pressing your lower back into the floor. Lift your legs 1 inch from the floor. Lift your shoulder blades an inch from the floor. This is the start. Now squeeze your abs, pulling your knees toward your chest. Return to the start. That’s 1 rep; do 25.

Reverse Lunge

Start standing, abs and glutes tight. Step back with your left leg, then bend at the knees, lowering your torso until your right thigh is parallel to the floor. Press through your right heel and return to the start. That’s 1 rep; do 10 on each side.
