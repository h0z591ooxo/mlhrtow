But even though the couple had been together for more than 10 years, Johnson says that he still felt some trepidation when it came time to walk down the aisle for the second time.

"My divorce did a number on me," Johnson told WSJ Magazine. "I wasn’t fearful of getting married again, there was just some hesitancy. But Lauren was incredibly patient: 'I love you, you love me, we have this amazing life together — no presh.'"

Johnson was married to producer Dany Garcia from 1997-2007, and they have one daughter together. The wrestler-turned-actor also explained that he wasn't looking to be in another relationship when he met Hashian, but he felt a connection to her from the start: "At the time, I was going through my breakup with Dany and [Hashian] was just coming off a big breakup, too. Ironically, when you’re not looking for something, the power of the universe kind of takes over."

However, Johnson and Garcia are on incredibly good terms—he's represented by her media management firm, The Garcia Companies, and they worked together on the upcoming Athleticon, a fitness and entertainment event that will take place in Atlanta next year.
