Never mind what you've seen at Denny's—fat guys skip breakfast. University of Massachusetts researchers recently discovered that men who don't eat in the a.m. are 4.5 times more likely to be obese than those who make it an everyday habit. In fact, during the year-long study, the men who reported missing breakfast just one time increased their obesity risk by 34 percent. "Eating breakfast is like putting kindling on the fire of your metabolism," says Gay Riley, R.D., a nutritionist in Dallas, Texas, and author of The Pocket Personal Trainer. And timing matters: Make sure you eat your first meal within an hour and a half of waking. The scientists found that guys who waited longer than that increased the likelihood they'd become heavyweights by 50 percent.
