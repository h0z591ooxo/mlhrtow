Modafinil and armodafinil are wakefulness-promoting medications commonly prescribed to treat excessive daytime sleepiness in people with narcolepsy. These stimulant-like drugs help improve alertness and reduce sudden sleep attacks, making them a first-line treatment option for managing narcolepsy symptoms.

However, excessive daytime sleepiness isn't always caused by narcolepsy—it can also stem from sleep apnea, thyroid issues, depression, or other treatable conditions. Common Medications

Modafinil and Armodafinil: These are usually the first choice for treating sleepiness. They help people stay awake by changing the chemicals in the brain that control sleep and wakefulness.

Amphetamine-like Stimulants: Sometimes, doctors use medications similar to amphetamines. These can help with alertness but might have more side effects.
