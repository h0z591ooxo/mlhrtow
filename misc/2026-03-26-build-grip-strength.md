# Build Grip Strength

• Hold a 10-, 25- or 45-pound Olympic weight plate in each hand, grasping the plate's ridge with your four fingers. • Press your thumb against the flat side of the plate. Stand with your arms at your sides and your palms inward. • Slowly straighten your fingers to lower the plates, the close your fingers again to raise the plates as high as possible. • Try for 10 repetitions. (Be sure to wipe the sweat off your hands first, or the plate is liable to slip and do something bad to your big toe.)

• Find a broomstick handle or a pipe at least 8 inches long and drill a hole in the center of it. Tie one end of a three-foot rope through the hole, and tie a 2 1/2 pound barbell plate to the other end. • Hold both ends of the stick, standing with your feet shoulder-width apart and your arms out in front of you. Keep your forearms parallel to the floor with your palms down. Roll the stick so that the rope wraps around it, pulling the weight upward. • Continue until the weight reaches the top of the stick, then unroll slowly. Repeat two more times. Add 1 1/4 pounds whenever the exercise becomes too easy.
