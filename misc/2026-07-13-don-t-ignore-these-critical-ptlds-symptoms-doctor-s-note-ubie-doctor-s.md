What are the symptoms of Post-Treatment Lyme Disease Syndrome (PTLDS)?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Post-Treatment Lyme Disease Syndrome (PTLDS) is a condition that can develop after standard antibiotic treatment for Lyme disease. Common PTLDS symptoms include persistent fatigue, joint and muscle pain, and cognitive difficulties such as brain fog or trouble concentrating. These symptoms can last for months and significantly impact daily life.

Explanation

Post-Treatment Lyme Disease Syndrome (PTLDS) occurs after someone has been treated for Lyme disease, but still experiences symptoms. These symptoms can last for months or even years.

Common Symptoms of PTLDS Include:

Fatigue: People often feel very tired and have low energy levels.

Pain: There can be ongoing aches in muscles and joints.

Cognitive Issues: Some people have trouble thinking clearly, concentrating, or remembering things.
