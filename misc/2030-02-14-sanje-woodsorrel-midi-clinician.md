Sanje Woodsorrel, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

A passion for access and inclusion: “I am a Family Nurse Practitioner with many years of experience in primary care and sexual health. I began my career working in low-income clinics, working closely with diverse communities, including the LGBTQ+ population.”

The importance of partnership: “I always aim to be working in partnership with my patients. I come with my experience and expertise, and the patient comes with their knowledge about themselves and their bodies. Together, we can find that common ground of the approach that's going to work best for them.”

Aging with options: “My passion for women’s and sexual health drives everything I do. I love that at Midi, we’re challenging outdated beliefs about aging and offering options to feel better. Aging doesn’t have to mean suffering, and I believe wholeheartedly in finding solutions together that allow women to thrive in all phases of life.”
