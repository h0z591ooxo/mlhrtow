Can Biotin injections help with hair loss?

Yes, Biotin is known for promoting healthier, fuller hair by boosting keratin production. While it may not reverse all types of hair loss, regular injections can help improve hair thickness, reduce thinning, and support overall scalp health.

Is there a risk of taking too much Biotin?

Since biotin is a water-soluble vitamin, your body will naturally excrete any excess amounts, making it difficult to overdose. However, it's still important to follow the dosage guidelines provided by your healthcare professional.
