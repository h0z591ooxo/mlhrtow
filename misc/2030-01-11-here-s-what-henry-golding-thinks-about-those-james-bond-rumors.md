# Here's What Henry Golding Thinks About Those James Bond Rumors

[Golding Thinks](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/)

[James Bond Rumors](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/9798147739.html)

There have been rumors about who will the next actor to play James Bond for years.

Possible picks include Henry Golding, Idris Elba, and Tom Hiddleston.

Golding addressed the rumors during an appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon.

[Golding Thinks About Those](https://bioreg.wiki/qrfs4r2v2b/)

While Daniel Craig is still wooing women and going on missions around the world as our current James Bond, there's been speculation about who will take the role over from him for years. Craig is reportedly still under contract for one more Bond film after next year's No Time to Die is released, but that hasn't stopped guys like Harry Styles and Chris Hemsworth from throwing their hats in the ring—and of course, there was a point where everyone and their mother wanted Idris Elba as Bond. In fact, Pierce Brosnan, a former Bond himself, wants a female James Bond.

Months ago, Jimmy Fallon predicted that Henry Golding would be the next Bond, and in another recent appearance on Fallon's show, Fallon brought up the casting rumors again. Apparently, Vegas betters believe that there are 10-1 odds that Golding will the next Bond, and, Golding seemed excited about possibly joining the iconic franchise.

As for his toughest competition? That would be the aforementioned Elba. "Who wants to see Elba kick some butt? He's amazing," Golding said. Golding continued, "If you're a male movie star, it is literally the most iconic role possible. I mean, you'd be an absolute idiot not to think and aspire for a role like that."

So basically, he's interested in the role, but doesn't want to seem over eager. Very smooth, that Golding.
