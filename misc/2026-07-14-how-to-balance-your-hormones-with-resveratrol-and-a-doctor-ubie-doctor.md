What Is Resveratrol?

Resveratrol is a polyphenol found in red grapes, berries, peanuts and red wine. It's known for:

Antioxidant effects: Neutralizes harmful free radicals

Anti-inflammatory action: Supports cellular health

Sirtuin activation: May improve metabolic and hormonal pathways

When used appropriately, resveratrol for hormonal balance can be part of a multi-pronged plan to optimize estrogen, insulin, cortisol and other key hormones.

Integrating Resveratrol into a Hormone-Balancing Plan

Nutrition

Working Closely with Your Doctor

Even natural supplements can interact with medications or underlying health issues. Discuss:

Current hormone levels (blood tests for estrogen, testosterone, thyroid, cortisol, insulin)

Medication list (e.g., blood thinners, hormone therapies)

Potential side effects (headache, digestive upset)

Long-term monitoring plan (repeat labs every 3–6 months)

A medical professional's oversight ensures safety and effectiveness.
