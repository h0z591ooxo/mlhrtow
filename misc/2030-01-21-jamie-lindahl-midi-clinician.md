Jamie Lindahl, BSN, APRN, WHNP-BC

Decades of dedication to women’s health: "I have been in healthcare since 1994, specializing in women’s health. I’ve wanted to care for others for as long as I can remember, and throughout my career I’ve worked in many different capacities. I’ve been a full-time and part-time clinician, held management roles, and worked in clinical quality and risk management, but the best part of my job has always been caring for patients."

Making menopause mainstream: "It’s a real privilege to work with women during a time in their lives when there hasn’t historically been much information or support available. Too often, women’s health, especially perimenopausal and menopausal health, is not prioritized. Reliable, evidence-based information can be hard to find. Being able to talk with women, normalize what they’re feeling, and help them determine the best path forward is incredibly meaningful, and I’m grateful to be part of that process."

Approaching healthcare as a team: "I view healthcare as a true partnership with my patients. I want to fully understand their concerns, medical conditions, and overall lives so I can provide the best possible information and guidance. My goal is to help them make decisions that not only address their symptoms or health conditions but also align with their lifestyles, values, and goals so that the care plan is truly effective and sustainable. Being an advocate, a trusted source of information, and a safe place to receive the care they need is one of the greatest privileges of this career."
